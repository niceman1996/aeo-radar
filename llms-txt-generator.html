Free llms.txt Generator

llms.txt is an experimental community standard (llmstxt.org): a Markdown file at your site root that describes what your site is about and which pages matter, so AI assistants that support it can understand and cite your content.

Straight talk: Google Search does not use llms.txt, and adoption by major AI crawlers is still unproven. Treat it as a cheap, five-minute hedge — it can't hurt, and the file is also a great site summary for humans. See our own llms.txt as an example.
